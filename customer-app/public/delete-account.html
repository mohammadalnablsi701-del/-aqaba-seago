Delete your Aqaba SeaGo account

Customer accounts can be permanently deleted directly inside the Aqaba SeaGo app.

In the app

1Sign in to your customer account.
2Open Profile.
3Open Personal details.
4Choose Delete my account and confirm the permanent deletion prompts.

What deletion does

Your login identity is removed and the account is disabled permanently. Push subscriptions and native mobile notification tokens linked to the account are removed. Some booking, payment, fraud-prevention or accounting records may be retained only where reasonably required for operational, contractual or legal obligations.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app, contact support@aqabaseago.com from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion.
