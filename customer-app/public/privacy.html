Privacy Policy

Aqaba SeaGo is a marketplace for marine trips and related experiences in Aqaba. This policy explains the information used to operate customer accounts, bookings, tickets, support, notifications and platform security.

Information we process

We may process your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, booking details, trip selections, ticket references, payment references, support messages, notification preferences, device or browser information, and operational logs needed to provide and protect the service.

Why we use it

We use information to create and secure accounts, process and manage bookings, issue QR tickets, communicate trip updates, support cancellations and refunds, provide customer support, prevent abuse, maintain records and improve service reliability.

Sharing

Information necessary to fulfil a booking may be shared with the relevant trip operator. We may also use infrastructure, email, hosting, analytics, payment and security providers where needed to operate the platform. We do not sell customer data to advertisers.

Retention and account deletion

You can request permanent account deletion from the Aqaba SeaGo app under Profile → Personal details → Delete account. Login identity and direct account access are removed. Some booking, payment, fraud-prevention or accounting records may be retained where reasonably necessary for operational, contractual or legal obligations.

Security

We use access controls, authenticated API access, rate limits, security headers and other technical safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may update your contact details, control notification permissions through your device or browser, contact support about your information, and delete your customer account from within the app.

Contact

Privacy and account requests: support@aqabaseago.com
